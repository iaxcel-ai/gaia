# Gaia onboarding guide

A short guide for facility staff and district admins. It covers setting up, updating your facility's resources, and sending and answering referrals. It is written in English; a Kinyarwanda version is not available yet.

You need a username and password from your district admin. If you do not have one, ask them. There is no sign-up page.

## Logging in

1. Open Gaia in a web browser on a phone or computer.
2. Type your username and password. Press the eye button next to the password to check what you typed.
3. Press **Log in**. You stay logged in if you refresh the page, until your session ends after 8 hours or you press **Log out**.

Forgot your password? Ask your district admin to reset it. Open "Forgot password?" on the login page for a reminder.

## Update your facility's resources

Do this at the start of each shift, and whenever a count changes, so other facilities are not misled. Counts older than two hours are marked **Stale**.

1. Open **Dashboard**. Your facility is selected.
2. Under **Update your facility**, choose the type (bed, specialist or supply).
3. Type the number you have now. Zero is a valid answer.
4. Press **Update**. The card above shows the new number and "Updated just now".

## Send a referral

1. On the **Dashboard**, find the **Refer a patient** list. Each facility shows its latest beds, specialists and supplies.
2. If the patient needs a bed, a specialist or supplies, choose it under **Patient needs** to see only facilities that have it.
3. Press **Refer patient here** next to the facility you choose.
4. Check the **From** and **To** facilities and the resources available there.
5. Enter a **patient reference** (a file number or initials, not the full name), the **condition**, and the **resource needed**.
6. Press **Send referral**. The receiving facility is told straight away. If nobody there is connected, a text message is sent instead.

You can cancel a referral from the **Referrals** page while it is still pending.

## Answer a referral

A badge on **Referrals** in the top bar shows how many referrals are waiting for an answer.

1. Open **Referrals** and click a referral to see the condition, the resource needed, who sent it and when.
2. Press **Accept** if you can take the patient.
3. Press **Decline** if you cannot. Choose why (no bed, no specialist, no supplies, or other) and press **Confirm decline**. The reason is shown to the sender and in the district report.
4. After the patient has arrived and been treated, open the referral again and press **Complete**.

Only staff at the receiving facility can accept, decline or complete a referral.

## For district admins

**Staff accounts**

1. Open **Staff accounts**.
2. To add someone, enter a username, a password of at least 7 characters, their role and their facility, then press **Create account**. Give them their username and password in person or by phone.
3. To help someone who forgot their password, choose them under **Reset a password**, type a new password and press **Reset password**.
4. The **Registered staff** table lists every account in your district. Press **Deactivate** when someone leaves: they can no longer log in, and their history is kept. Press **Reactivate** to bring them back.

**District report**

Open **District report** for referral counts by status, the share of answered referrals that were declined, why they were declined, which facilities decline most, which facilities send and receive the most referrals, and resources that ran out more than once in the last 30 days.

## Dark mode

Press the moon button in the top bar to switch to dark mode, and the sun to switch back. Gaia remembers your choice on that device.
